About

Ruchi Sharma

Owner/Operator

Hi! I'm Ruchi Sharma and I approach my organizing with an eye on design (hence the name!). I have a business degree and MBA and worked in a Top 5 management consulting firm for years helping organizations create more efficient and organized systems and processes.

Over the years I've created organized spaces for my own household with 3 kids, extended family, and numerous friends. I have always had a knack for organizing whether it's companies or closets.

My Approach

I believe a space should not only be organized, but also visually appealing and welcoming. Every project starts with getting to know you and your family, then planning systems, zones, and storage that are easy to maintain and natural to use.

I can also help reimagine spaces that fit your lifestyle, like adding a coffee or wine bar, or turning a guest room into an office. The possibilities are endless.

What sets me apart

  • Judgment-free collaboration from start to finish
  • Design-forward solutions that still feel livable
  • Customized systems and labels for long-term success
  • Reuse-first planning and recycling-minded solutions using what you already have
  • Optional donation coordination to reduce waste

FAQ's

Answers to common questions.

How do I get started with OBD?

After you contact me, I provide a half-hour initial consultation at no charge. During this time, we review your space, discuss goals, share ideas, and go over my approach and fees. I usually do this virtually via FaceTime, Zoom, or phone using your photos and video clips, and if you're nearby, I'm happy to do a home visit.

How long does a typical organizing session take?

Most sessions run between 3-6 hours depending on the space and your goals, with a 3-hour minimum per session.

How much do you charge?

I mostly charge by the hour, and for some larger spaces I may charge by project. Factors like complexity and travel time are also considered. I typically start at $60/hour, which is lower than many local organizers.

Do I need to buy bins and containers before we start?

No. We start with what you already own, then recommend only the products that improve function and fit your style.

Can you help with decluttering decisions?

Yes. We guide you through a supportive, judgment-free process to help you decide what to keep, donate, recycle, or discard.

Do I have to be there?

While I prefer clients to make the keep and purge decisions, I can also work solo on many projects. We can discuss what works best during your initial consultation.

What areas do you serve?

I primarily serve the DMV area, including Washington, DC, Maryland, and Northern Virginia.
